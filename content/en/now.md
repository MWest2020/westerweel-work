This overview writes itself. Every project keeps its roadmap as data in its
own repository: the goal, the phases, and what "done" means for the current
phase. An agent factory on my own hardware (Ratatoskr and Habitat) reads that
every two hours and shows here, per project, the current phase, the progress
and what comes next. An item only counts as done with evidence. Nothing
sensitive is on this page: no secrets, no document content, no client names,
and private projects are not listed.
