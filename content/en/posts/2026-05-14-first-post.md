Short first post to explain why this site exists.

LinkedIn is for people who already know each other. GitHub is for code.
Neither is a place where you can calmly put down a thousand-word
thought and find it again a year later. That's still what a personal
blog is for.

### What goes here

- Notes from DevOps work: Kubernetes incidents, Proxmox builds, ArgoCD
  patterns that work or don't.
- ISO 27001 pieces I've rewritten for our own stack — the generic
  versions, not the client-specific ones.
- Grant work: what an NLnet application actually asks for, and what it
  doesn't.
- Occasionally something about the homelab.

### What doesn't

- Hot takes.
- AI-generated content marketing.
- Cross-posts from LinkedIn.
