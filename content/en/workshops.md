Slide decks I use for workshops, sessions and talks. They run entirely in your
browser — navigate with the arrow keys (`←` / `→`), space for the next slide,
`F` for fullscreen. On mobile: tap right to advance, left to go back.

## Decks

- **[Shift left — hooks > good intentions](/slides/claude-shift-left/)** —
  why CLAUDE.md doesn't enforce your rule and what does: hooks, permissions,
  and where a check belongs. With a [reading version](/slides/claude-shift-left/lees/)
  and [code + hand-out](https://github.com/MWest2020/westerweel-work/tree/main/workshops/claude-shift-left).
  Dev session, ~1 hour. *(Dutch)*
- **[Docker — from image to registry](/slides/docker-101/)** — the mental model
  behind containers, and why `docker commit` breaks your CI while a Dockerfile
  doesn't. Dev session, ~1 hour. *(Dutch)*
